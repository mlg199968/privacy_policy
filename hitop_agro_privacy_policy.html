Privacy Policy for Hitop Agro

Package Name: ir.mlggrand.hitopAgro
Developer: MLG Grand
Last Updated: November 25, 2026

MLG Grand ("we", "our", or "us") developed the Hitop Agro application as a commercial agricultural, inventory, and accounting management app. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use Hitop Agro.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Hitop Agro is designed with an offline-first architecture. Your business and agricultural records—including products/inventory, invoices, customer and farmer lists, accounting ledgers, cheques, expenses, farm details (including any farm coordinates you manually enter), field visits, soil/water/leaf test records, prescriptions, notes, shop details, and attached images—are stored locally on your device. We do not automatically upload or sell your business or customer records to our servers.

2. Information We Collect and Process

When you use online features such as account sign-in, subscription management, or notifications, the following information may be transmitted to our servers (mlggrand.ir / mlggrand2.ir):

  • Account & Contact Information: Your phone number (for OTP verification), full name, optional email address, and optional referral code. If you manage an organizational/team subscription, the phone numbers and names of team members you add are also processed for access management.
  • Device & Technical Information: A persistent app-generated device identifier, device brand, model, device name, operating system version, platform, and app version/build number—used to bind licenses/subscriptions to authorized devices and deliver compatible notifications.
  • App Usage & Telemetry Data: Basic session telemetry such as app launch count, active usage duration (periodic heartbeat pings), and notification interactions (read, dismissed, clicked) to maintain service reliability and license status.
  • Local Error Logs: Diagnostic error logs and stack traces are recorded locally on your device and are only shared if you manually choose to export and send them to support.

3. App Permissions & Their Purpose

  • Camera: Used solely to scan product barcodes/QR codes and capture photos of products, farms, visits, or documents at your request.
  • Storage / Photos & Media: Used to attach images/files to records, export PDF/Excel reports, and create or restore local backup files.
  • Bluetooth & Nearby Devices / Location (Android 11 and below): Used exclusively to discover and connect to Bluetooth thermal printers for printing invoices and receipts. The app does not track or collect your background GPS location.
  • Internet & Network/Wi-Fi State: Used for OTP authentication, subscription verification, receiving in-app notices, optional Google Drive backups, payment redirects, and Wi-Fi/network printing.

4. Third-Party Services

Hitop Agro integrates with the following third-party services only when you use the corresponding features:

  • Google Sign-In & Google Drive API (Optional Cloud Backup): If you choose to back up or restore your data using Google Drive, you authenticate directly with your Google account. Authentication tokens are stored securely on your device, and the app only accesses files it creates for backup/restore purposes. We do not access, sell, or share your other Google Drive files.
  • Payment Gateways (e.g., Zarinpal): When purchasing a subscription, you are redirected to an external payment gateway. We do not collect or store your bank card numbers, PINs, or payment credentials.
  • Google ML Kit Barcode Scanning: Processes barcode frames locally on your device for scanning.

5. Data Security & Retention

All communications with our servers use HTTPS encryption. Authentication credentials and tokens are stored in encrypted/secure local storage on your device. Account and subscription data are retained only as long as your account or subscription remains active or as required by applicable law.

6. Account & Data Deletion

You can delete local business data at any time from within the app or by uninstalling the app. To request permanent deletion of your account and server-side records (phone number, profile, and connected device records), please contact us at support@mlggrand.ir with your registered phone number. Requests are processed within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Children's Privacy

Hitop Agro is a business and agricultural management tool and is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
